## Decided
Use assets/astra-logo-harmonized.png for STYLE and assets/astra-logo-three-branches-symmetric.png for SHAPE because the user explicitly requested that combination after rejecting simplified flat treatments. Preserve three connected folded branches and a fully closed hexagonal heart. Apply the rich blue-white and amber star field, dimensional rim and white rounded-square app tile because those are the appearance the user wants to keep.

## Tried and ruled out
Solid navy fills, sparse symbolic stars and bright blue gradients removed the desired galaxy identity. The user corrected those trials by supplying the two references and asking to combine them. Do not interpret a request for a finished logo as permission to discard that finish.

## Watch out
The fusion is assets/astra-logo-three-branches-starry-v1.png. Its preview in test-results/astra-logo-options.html was visibly checked at 24, 32, 48, 64 and 128px. It remains a proposal, not a deployed or user-approved app replacement, because visual selection is still in progress. This reference-role lesson has direct task-specific evidence; transfer to a different design task is unverified.
