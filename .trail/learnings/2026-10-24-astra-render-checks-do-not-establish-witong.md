## Decided
The user rejected the 30-second, 16-cut demo generated by scripts/render-demo.ts and website/media/astra-demo.html because it felt too fast and fixed, and rejected the beige Focus Timer mini-app as unattractive. Preserve real native Astra footage and verify the generated app visually before investing in the final edit. Confirm spoken English independently of the interface language switch in website/site.ts or the native UI.

## Tried and ruled out
A permanent left headline and right product window, with repeated crop cuts, passed timing, text and audio review but failed the human creative review. More cuts did not create the requested energy. Switching the interface to English did not make the next voice session reply in English; an explicit spoken instruction and a confirmed English reply were needed.

## Watch out
Cue: replacing the public demo linked by website/index.html and README.md. Lesson: technical render checks do not replace review of the actual mini-app, composition, motion and spoken language. Evidence: the user rejected the technically passing split-screen render on 2026-10-24. A full-frame Daylight dashboard and a real voice-added focus mode were captured afterwards; that alternative is a proposal until the user accepts its look and storyboard. Do not claim a general improvement from this correction alone.
